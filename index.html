A page about Dean's Favorite Mountain

Mt. Denali is the tallest mountain in North America and the tallest land base to summit mountain in the world.

Denali is about 60 million years old. Only about 500 people summit Denali per year and is said to be harder to summit than Mt. Everest

Three facts about Denali

  • It generates it's own weather making the summit only available about 30% of the time.
  • The winter patrols on the mountain is mainly led by sled dogs.
  • Is on of the 7 summits of the world. The 7 summits consist of the Tallest mountains from each continent.
